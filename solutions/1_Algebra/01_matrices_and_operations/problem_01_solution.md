# Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

## Question

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Tasks: (1) state the sizes of matrices $A$ and $B$; (2) read off the entries $a_{12}$, $a_{23}$, $b_{21}$, and $b_{32}$; (3) write the second row of $A$ and the first column of $B$ as vectors.

## Solution

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## 1. Sizes

- Size of $A$: $2\times 3$ (2 rows, 3 columns).
- Size of $B$: $3\times 2$ (3 rows, 2 columns).

## 2. Entries

The entry $a_{ij}$ is in row $i$ and column $j$.

$$
a_{12}=-1,\quad a_{23}=5,\quad b_{21}=-2,\quad b_{32}=1
$$

## 3. Row and column

Second row of $A$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

First column of $B$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
-2 \\
4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
