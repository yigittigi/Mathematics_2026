# Exercise 6. Parameter and Invertibility

## Question

For

$$
A(t)=
\begin{pmatrix}
t & 1 \\
2 & t \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

find all values of $t$ for which the matrix is not invertible. Explain how your answer is related to the determinant being zero.

## Solution

$$
\det A(t)=t\cdot t-1\cdot2=t^2-2
$$

The matrix is not invertible exactly when its determinant is zero:

$$
t^2-2=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\pm\sqrt{2}
$$

For these values the determinant is zero; for all other real values of $t$ the matrix is invertible.
