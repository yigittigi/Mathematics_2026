# Exercise 1. Determinant of a 2×2 Matrix

## Question

Compute

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.

## Solution

For a $2\times2$ matrix, $\det A=ad-bc$.

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix}=3\cdot4-(-2)\cdot5=12+10=22
$$

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14 \\
\end{pmatrix}=1\cdot14-7\cdot2=0
$$

Only the first matrix is invertible, because a square matrix is invertible if and only if its determinant is nonzero.
